# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters and engineering leads, in Indonesia and abroad, who are hiring for Java or backend roles. They open the link from a CV, a LinkedIn message, or a job application, usually on a laptop during the working day, sometimes on a phone between meetings. They are comparing several candidates and give each one a short look. The job to be done: understand within seconds who Sarip is, what he has worked on, for how long, and how to reach him or get his CV.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Sarip Hidayatullah, a Java developer in Jakarta (since 2021: PT. Lemurian Inovasi Teknologi, then PT. Prawathiya Karsa Pradiptha). It shows real experience, real client and internal projects, education, and contact details, and offers the CV as a PDF. Success: a reader leaves with an accurate picture of his experience and either downloads the CV or sends an email.

## Brand Personality

Calm, meticulous, plain-spoken. The site should read like the work of an engineer who checks things twice: nothing invented, nothing inflated, every detail correct. Quiet overall, with one confident moment per page. English copy, short sentences, no marketing voice.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI-generated developer portfolios: dark background with neon or purple gradients, glass cards, glowing borders, typing-text effects, skill progress bars, "passionate developer" copy, floating tech-logo clouds.
- Template portfolio structure: hero with two CTAs, identical project cards with stock thumbnails, fake stats ("50+ projects"), testimonials that do not exist.
- Clones of well-known product sites (Linear, Vercel, Apple) and terminal costumes for a person who is not selling a CLI.

## Design Principles

1. Nothing invented. Every project, date, and claim comes from Sarip's real history; if a detail is unknown, it is left out, not filled in.
2. Scannable in ten seconds. A recruiter should find role, years, current employer, and contact without scrolling hunts.
3. Calm, with one strong moment. Restraint everywhere, then a single deliberate accent where attention matters.
4. Careful is visible. Precise alignment, correct type, working links, readable contrast: the craft itself is the evidence of how he works.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 text contrast, 3:1 for UI boundaries and focus indicators, full keyboard use with visible focus, 44px touch targets. Every animation has a `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative. Light and dark themes both meet the same bar.
